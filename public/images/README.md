# Image guidance (read before adding anything)

## Allowed sources
- Unsplash (unsplash.com/license) and Pexels (pexels.com/license): free for
  commercial use, no attribution required. Search: "american house exterior",
  "suburban home", "front porch", "for sale sign".
- Photos you or a partner investor took, with permission.

## NOT allowed
- Images pulled from Google Images, Zillow, Redfin, realtor listings or
  competitor sites. These are copyrighted; a DMCA claim can take the site down.

## Captioning rule
Stock photos are decoration, never evidence. Do NOT caption a stock image as a
property you sold or bought. Once a real deal closes, ask the investor for a
photo of that property plus written permission, then caption it honestly:
  "Bought in 14 days — Frayser, Memphis, March 2026"

## How to use
Drop files here as: hero-us.jpg, hero-uk.jpg, hero-ca.jpg, hero-au.jpg
(1600x900 or larger). The hero background activates automatically per country
once the matching file exists — no code changes needed.
